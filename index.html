01 — The StudioNEW DELHI

Resin, flowers and a photograph that refuses to fade.

The Giftè Memora is a handcrafted resin art studio. We take the things that usually get lost — a wedding bouquet, a first photograph, a baby's umbilical cord, a song you played on repeat — and set them in clear resin so they stop being fragile.

Nothing here is mass produced. Each piece is poured, cured, sanded and polished by hand, which is why a keychain takes five to seven days and a wedding frame takes ten to fourteen. Slight variation between pieces is not a defect; it is the signature of something made once, for one person. Our work is built to be an heirloom, not a season.

05 — Questions & PolicyFAQ
How long does an order take?

Seven to fourteen days to handcraft, then three to five days in transit. Keychains and small spiritual stands are faster at five to seven days.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in India. Delivery is free on orders over ₹1,299, and orders over ₹1,999 include a free gift.

How do I pay?

By UPI, after the design is confirmed. Nothing is poured before you approve it.

Can everything be customised?

Yes. Photos, names, dates, colours, dried flowers, Spotify codes and LED backlighting are all available. Customisation details are needed up front, before production starts.

What if it arrives damaged?

Email us within forty-eight hours of delivery with photographs and we will make it right. Because each piece is made to order, we cannot refund after production has begun.

Do you take bulk or corporate orders?

Yes, and bulk pricing is lower than the listed rates. Send the quantity and the brief and we will quote it.
